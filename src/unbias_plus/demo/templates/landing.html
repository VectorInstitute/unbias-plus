How We Define Bias UnBias-Plus defines bias as language patterns that systematically favour or disadvantage particular groups, viewpoints, or interpretations. Our model reflects one rigorous approach to bias detection, primarily trained on English-language journalistic content with expert annotation. We recognize that bias is complex, context-dependent, and culturally situated, and that our definition may not capture every perspective. We are committed to continuously learning and expanding the tool to encompass broader and more inclusive forms of bias over time.
